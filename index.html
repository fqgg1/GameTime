Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026
The Short Version: GameTime is designed with privacy as its top priority. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal or usage data. Everything happens 100% locally on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to GameTime ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application functions regarding your data.

2. Zero Data Collection & Cloud Storage

Because GameTime operates entirely on-device:

  • We do not own, rent, or operate remote servers to store your usage data.
  • We do not transmit your screen time stats, app lists, or habits over the internet.
  • We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, IP address, or location.

3. How Your Data Is Processed

GameTime requires specific permissions (such as Usage Stats or Accessibility Services) strictly to track your screen time and present it to you within the app. All calculated statistics, logs, and tracking data are written directly to your device’s local storage (e.g., local database or preferences).

4. Third-Party Services

Since no data leaves your device, we do not integrate with third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers that track your activity across apps or websites.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your screen time data at all times:

  • Clearing Data: You can clear the app’s stored data anytime through your device's System Settings.
  • Uninstalling: Deleting GameTime from your device immediately and permanently removes all data generated by the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we comply fully with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar international regulations by default.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top. However, our core commitment—that your screen time data stays entirely on your device—will remain unchanged.

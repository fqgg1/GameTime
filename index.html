Android · Private by design

Screen time that stays
on your phone.

GameTime shows where your time goes and helps you stick to limits you set yourself. No account, no cloud, and your usage data never leaves your phone.

What it does

Daily & weekly usage

See time per app for today, this week, last week or last month, plus unlock and notification counts.

Per-app limits

Set a different limit for each day of the week. When time is up, the app is blocked.

App groups

Put apps together (like all your games) and share one combined limit.

Focus mode

Pick distracting apps and cap their total time for the day.

PIN & bonus time

Protect your settings with a PIN and grant yourself extra time deliberately, not by accident.

Widgets & themes

Home-screen widgets, light/dark/auto mode and four colour themes: Midnight, Sakura, Forest and Lavender.

Your privacy, plainly

  • Internet is only for ads. GameTime uses the internet only to show ads, and they never affect how the app works. Your screen time and app usage are not sent anywhere.
  • No accounts and no usage tracking. Nothing to sign up for, and no analytics on what you do in the app.
  • Your data stays on-device. Limits, groups, PIN and preferences are stored locally on your phone.
  • It never reads what’s on your screen. The accessibility service only learns which app just opened, so it can enforce limits. It can’t see text, messages or passwords.
  • You’re in control. Every permission is optional to revoke in Android settings, and uninstalling removes everything. Read the full privacy policy.

Permissions, and why

PermissionWhy GameTime needs it
Usage accessLets Android tell GameTime how long each app was open. This is the core of the app.
Accessibility serviceDetects the moment an app launches so a limit can be enforced instantly. Window content is not read.
Display over other appsShows the “time’s up” block screen on top of a limited app.
InternetOnly used to load ads.
Notifications & foreground serviceKeeps the monitor running reliably and shows its status notification.
